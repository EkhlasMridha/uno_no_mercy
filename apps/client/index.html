UNO No Mercy

The 168-card shedding game that hits back, played free in your browser. Stack draws up to +10, swap hands on a 7, and drop out the moment you hold 25 cards. Bring 2 to 20 friends, or fill the table with computer players.

How to play UNO No Mercy

Everyone is dealt 7 cards. Play a card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol, or a wild. If you can't play, keep drawing until you can. Answer a Draw card with one of equal or higher value to stack it on to the next player. Anyone who reaches 25 cards is knocked out by the Mercy Rule. Empty your hand, or be the last player standing, to win.

The action cards

Skip, Reverse, Draw 2, Draw 4, Discard All, Skip Everyone, Wild Reverse Draw 4, Wild Draw 6, Wild Draw 10 and Wild Color Roulette.
